SubSentry is a Java-based subscription tracking and expense monitoring system. 
It will allow users to manage their subscriptions, track their costs, monitor renewal dates,
 and manage their subscription statuses.

Many people use multiple subscription services, but managing them individually makes it difficult 
to keep track of recurring payments, renewal dates, subscription status, and overall spending. 
This can lead to forgotten subscriptions and poor visibility of recurring expenses.
SubSentry is designed to provide one centralized system for managing and monitoring subscriptions.
